Coach Feedback

Early — Objection"I'm busy."

Auto Repair

When someone's rushing in and says they don't have time for a full inspection, we always say — the quick check now beats the tow truck later. Two minutes now, real savings starting next month.

Restaurant

It's like a to-go order — you don't need the full sit-down experience, just handed to you fast. One document and I'm out of your hair.

Service (Salon/Spa)

Like a client asking for a quick trim between appointments. This takes less time than that — not an hour, just a two-minute favor.

Retail

Like a customer doing a quick return at the counter, in and out. Thirty seconds to grab a statement.

Early — Objection"Not interested."

Auto Repair

Think of it like a free second opinion before a big repair — not committing to the shop, just finding out if the first diagnosis was right.

Restaurant

Think of it like a blind taste test. Doesn't cost you anything to find out if there's a better dish out there.

Service (Salon/Spa)

It's like a free consultation before a big treatment. Costs nothing to know your options.

Retail

It's like comparison shopping before a big purchase — doesn't commit you to anything, just gives you the facts.

Early — Objection"I'm happy with my current processor."

Auto Repair

Even a car running fine still gets an inspection every year, just to make sure nothing's hiding under the hood.

Restaurant

Even a five-star kitchen still gets health inspections — not because something's wrong, just to confirm everything's tight.

Service (Salon/Spa)

Even a great stylist still does a color consultation now and then, just to confirm the formula's still the best fit.

Retail

Even a store doing great numbers still audits its vendor contracts once a year, just to be sure they're not overpaying.

Early — Objection"A friend / family member does my processing."

Auto Repair

It's like getting a second mechanic to check the brakes — doesn't mean you don't trust your guy, just confirms he's got you dialed in right.

Restaurant

It's just a gut-check — like having a friend food critic taste-test the menu. If everything's great, you'll know for sure instead of just hoping.

Service (Salon/Spa)

Like getting a second opinion on a treatment plan from another provider — doesn't mean you leave your guy, just confirms he's taking care of you.

Retail

Like getting a second quote on insurance even when you like your agent. Worst case, you find out you're already getting the best deal.

Early — Objection"I get calls like this all the time."

Auto Repair

Difference is I'm not asking you to believe me — I'm asking to run the equivalent of a compression test instead of just taking my word the engine's fine.

Restaurant

This isn't a pitch, it's a taste test with your actual numbers instead of my sales pitch.

Service (Salon/Spa)

This is different — like a patch test before a full treatment. We're not guessing, we're checking your actual numbers.

Retail

Most of those calls want you to trust them blind. I'm asking you to trust your own statement instead of my pitch.

Early — Objection"I already pay zero." (cash discount / zero-fee program)

Auto Repair

Not all zero-fee setups are tuned the same, kind of like two shops both saying "lifetime warranty" with very different fine print. Worth confirming yours is running clean.

Restaurant

I've seen kitchens on "zero fee" programs still get hit with junk fees buried in the statement, like a $9.99 item that rings up $11. Let's just check the math.

Service (Salon/Spa)

I've seen "zero fee" programs still nickel-and-dime with monthly minimums or PCI fees, like a package that says "unlimited" but caps at four visits.

Retail

Zero-fee doesn't always mean truly zero — like "no interest" financing that still has a fee buried in it. Worth confirming yours is clean.

Late-callTrust and commitment objections

The Staller — agreeable, avoids commitment

Wins by giving a specific, physical next action (portal login, a name to email, a text) and locking a time — not by trying harder to convince them.

The Guarded Owner — privacy-hesitant, been burned before

Wins by normalizing the ask without getting defensive and clarifying only the fee page is needed, not banking details.

The Ghost — says yes, low intent to follow through

Wins by refusing a vague promise and offering a backup path (portal, bookkeeper) before the call ends.
